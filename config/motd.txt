<h1>Welcome to Nova Station 13!</h1>

<p>
Welcome aboard and thank you for joining <b>Nova Station 13</b>, a community-driven server built by the 
<b>TAA Gaming Community</b>. Whether you are a veteran crew member or completely new to the world of 
Space Station 13, we are glad to have you here and hope you enjoy your time on the station.
</p>

<h2>About This Server</h2>

<p>
Nova Station 13 is currently running a <b>modified version of the Baystation12 SS13 codebase</b>. 
The Baystation12 project is one of the most well-known and respected codebases within the 
Space Station 13 community, known for its deep roleplay mechanics, immersive gameplay systems, 
and complex station simulation. Our server builds upon that foundation while introducing our 
own custom changes, features, and improvements over time.
</p>

<p>
For more information about the original project, you can visit:
<br>
<a href="https://baystation12.net/">https://baystation12.net/</a>
</p>

<h2>Server Development Status</h2>

<p>
Please be aware that <b>Nova Station 13 is currently a Work In Progress (WIP)</b>. Many systems, 
features, and mechanics are still under active development. Because of this, you may occasionally 
experience bugs, unfinished content, or experimental features that are still being tested.
</p>

<p>
Our goal is to build a stable, fun, and community-focused SS13 environment. As development 
continues, the server will receive regular updates, improvements, balance changes, and new content.
Your patience and feedback during this early phase are greatly appreciated.
</p>

<h2>Server Rules (Temporary Notice)</h2>

<p>
Since Nova Station 13 is a brand new server, we do not yet have a finalized and fully documented 
rule set in place. However, we ask all players to follow a few basic guidelines until the official 
rules are published.
</p>

<ul>
<li>Use common sense when interacting with other players.</li>
<li>Respect fellow crew members and staff.</li>
<li>Do not intentionally grief, exploit, or ruin the gameplay experience for others.</li>
<li>Play fair and help maintain a fun and welcoming environment.</li>
<li>In simple terms: <b>Don't be a jerk.</b></li>
</ul>

<p>
Once the server grows and development progresses further, a full rule system and moderation 
structure will be introduced.
</p>
